Quick Tips

Simple techniques for everyday wellness.

🌬️ Box Breathing Technique

Box breathing is a simple technique used by Navy SEALs:

  1. Inhale for 4 seconds
  2. Hold for 4 seconds
  3. Exhale for 4 seconds
  4. Hold for 4 seconds

Repeat 4 times for optimal calming effect.

🌿 Progressive Muscle Relaxation

Tense and release muscle groups from toes to head:

  1. Tense a muscle group for 5 seconds
  2. Release and notice the relaxation for 10 seconds
  3. Move to the next muscle group
☀️ Morning Routine Ideas

Start your day with intention:

  • Avoid phone for first 30 minutes
  • Drink a glass of water
  • 5 minutes of stretching or movement
  • Write 3 things you're grateful for
  • Set one meaningful intention for the day

⚠️ Important: TheraVox is not a substitute for professional mental health care. If you're experiencing persistent difficulties, please consult a qualified mental health professional. The tools and resources here are meant to complement, not replace, professional support.
